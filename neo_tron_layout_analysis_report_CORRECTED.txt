  • Redirects improve substantially.

PHYSICAL / FSPEED MODEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Orthogonal equal grid, Planck-like.
• Movement is measured from the last key pressed by that finger, not from home.
• Squared grid distance is used: dx^2 + dy^2.
• Direct SFB weight = 1.0.
• Skip-1 / DSFB ratio = 0.11.
• Skip-2 and skip-3 weights = 0.055 and 0.0275.
• Finger-strength multipliers: index 1.00, middle 1.10, ring 1.25, pinky 1.45.
• Red and Blue are one-shot thumb layers with equal low access cost.
• Purple requires two presses.
• Layer access returns immediately to base after the symbol.
• NICOLA dakuten is modeled as cross-hand thumb shift.

NOTE ON FSPEED:
The corpus supplies aggregate bigram/skipgram frequencies, not the original full
keystroke stream. The calculation therefore implements the weighted SFB/SFS Fspeed
model from those aggregate n-grams. It cannot reconstruct every intervening keypress
for skip-2/skip-3 sequences.

BOTTOM LINE — NEO-TRON
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Neo-TRON's strongest corrected gains over original TRON are lower SFB, lower SFS,
higher alternation, and lower redirects. Total rolling is essentially similar/slightly
higher, but Neo has fewer inrolls and more outrolls. Its main cost is Fspeed: once
blank physical positions are preserved correctly, the weighted movement index is
slightly worse than original TRON despite the lower raw same-finger frequencies.
