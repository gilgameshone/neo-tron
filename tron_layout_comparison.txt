SUMMARY OF THE LAYOUT CHANGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• neo-tron lowers SFBs from 6.905% to 6.609% (4.3% relative reduction).
• neo-tron lowers DSFBs from 9.865% to 9.729% (1.4% relative reduction).
• Total scissors increase from 3.547% to 3.967% (+11.8% relative).
• However, the specifically important inward-top scissor direction falls from 2.088% to 2.008% (3.8% relative reduction).
• The increase in total scissors is therefore driven by the reverse/outward-top direction, which rises from 1.460% to 1.959% (+34.2% relative).
• Left-hand load moves from 39.546% to 41.046% of mapped characters, making neo-tron less right-heavy.

METRIC DEFINITIONS / LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
SFB: adjacent corpus bigram where two different keys use the same finger.
DSFB: skipgram A?B where A and B use the same finger on different keys.
Scissors: same-hand top↔bottom movement using adjacent fingers or fingers
          one apart; this matches the scissor family discussed for TRON.
Lateral stretch proxy: same-row, same-hand bigram separated by >=2 columns.
Pinky–ring: same-hand bigram between pinky and ring fingers.
Trigram categories are based on hand alternation and inward/outward finger
rank. They are not claimed to be byte-for-byte equivalent to the analyser
shown in the screenshot because its exact scoring/category implementation
was not supplied.

For an exact reproduction of that analyser’s Fspeed, Score, Bad Redirects,
Sft, etc., its metric definitions/source or executable would be required.
